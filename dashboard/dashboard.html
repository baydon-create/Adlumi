Welcome to Adlumi 👋

Browse Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Reddit, X or Google Search as usual — every ad you scroll past is saved here automatically with its copy, creative, CTA and landing page. On the Meta Ad Library you get "days running" badges and one-click saving.

More filters

Search the Meta Ad Library

Searches Meta's official Ad Library API with your own access token (add it in Settings). Meta only returns commercial ads that were delivered in the EU/UK; political & issue ads are returned for every country. For anything else, use the Ad Library website — Adlumi adds save buttons there.

Market intelligence

Scope the market by keyword, platform or industry, then see who dominates spend, which ads are winning, and which angles, offers, formats and hooks over-index in winners. Spend and cost figures are modelled ranges. Open any ad to see exactly how they were estimated.

Meta Ad Library API

Create a token in the Graph API Explorer after confirming your identity at facebook.com/ID. The token is stored only in this browser.

Estimation model

Spend, CPM, CPA and revenue for competitor ads are modelled from public signals plus benchmarks. Set your market and, if you know your niche's real numbers, override the benchmarks to sharpen every estimate.
