See which competitor ads are actually winning

Adlumi saves ads from the Meta Ad Library and your feeds into one searchable library, shows how long each ad has been running, and turns them into market insights you can act on.

🔥 Days running, at a glance

Every ad in the Ad Library gets a "running N days" badge. Long-running ads are the ones making money.

🗂️ One library for every ad

Save ads with one click or capture them automatically as you browse, including copy, creative, CTA, landing page and engagement.

⚡ Winning score

Ranks ads by longevity, variations, engagement growth and reach, so the best ideas rise to the top.

📊 Market intelligence

Share of voice, the angles, offers, hooks and formats that over-index in winning ads, launch cadence and audience data.

🔬 Landing page insights

Detect store platform, tracking pixels, apps, prices and offers on a competitor's landing page.

🔒 Private by design

Your research stays in your browser. No account, no tracking, no data sent to us.

How it works

  1. Add Adlumi to Chrome.
  2. Search a competitor or keyword in the Meta Ad Library and click Save all visible, or just browse your feeds.
  3. Open the dashboard to filter, compare and export what's working.

Spend, CPM and CPA figures are modelled estimates built from public signals, shown as ranges with a confidence level. Use them to compare competitors, not as exact numbers.
